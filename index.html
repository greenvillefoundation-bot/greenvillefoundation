About Us

Our Story

The organization “GreenVille Foundation” Irengbam Makha Maning Leikai, Bishnupur District, Manipur was conceived in the year 2004, 15th April by a group of like-minded residents to resilient a thorough upliftment of the people of the Village inclusive neighbouring villages to cater all sorts of talents and skills of both indigenous and nonindigenous that the people of the District possess in the field of industries, arts, education, farming, environmental conservation, health care, theatre, promotion of new technology, library, agriculture/horticulture, science, establishment of required common facility centres, exhibitions, rehabilitation centres, differently able upliftment, women & child welfare, computer centre, information centre and mass communication, sports, food processing, cold storage and all kind of training centre for research, leadership, pisciculture, animal husbandry etc. as reflected in the Memorandum of Association of the organization.

After prolonged sensitisation and motivation of the people of the village and the adjoining villages of the district, it was agreed upon to establish one voluntary organization for the benefit of the people of the village and district as a whole. Accordingly, a general meeting was convened on 9th Sept. 2004 at the Irengbam Makha Maning Leikai to sort out the aims and objects for which the proposed organization is established and the name to be adopted for registration to the Govt. of Manipur.

Based on the final decision, 20 number of aims and objects had been chalked out and approved by the attending villagers for the all-round benefits of the District. It was agreed upon that the name should be ‘GreenVille Foundation” as proposed by the presenting village people, and shall be non-political, gender free, and not based on caste & creed, religions etc. so as to cover more members for the betterment of the village and District as a whole. Accordingly, the society/organization was registered to Govt. of Manipur on 20th, June 2005 bearing Registration No. 223 in the Bishnupur Registrar Office, Bishnupur District Manipur to look after all affairs of the society.

Our Impact Area

Based in the beautiful Bishnupur District of Manipur, GreenVille Foundation works across multiple villages and communities in the region. Our focus is on creating sustainable solutions that respect the local culture and environment while addressing pressing ecological challenges.

We collaborate with local farmers, schools, and community leaders to implement programs that benefit both people and the planet. From the shores of Loktak Lake to the hills of Bishnupur, our work is helping to preserve Manipur's natural heritage for future generations.
